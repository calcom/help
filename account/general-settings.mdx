## Language, timezone and time format

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Language | The language of the Cal.com app and the emails you receive. |
| Timezone | Your default timezone. Bookers always see times in their own timezone. |
| Time format | 12-hour or 24-hour. This only changes how times show inside Cal.com, not on your public booking pages. |
| Start of week | The first day of the week in calendars and availability views. |

Click **Update** to save.

## Travel schedule

If you're travelling, you can schedule a timezone change so your availability follows you without editing your schedules.

1. Click **Schedule timezone change**.
2. Pick the **Time range** of your trip, or turn on **Schedule timezone without end date** and pick a start date.
3. Choose the **Timezone** you'll be in and click **Add**.

Scheduled trips are listed under **Travel schedule**. Click **Delete** to remove one.

## Account-wide options

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Limit booking frequency | Sets the most bookings you can receive per day, week, month or year across all your personal and team booking links. Shown for organization members. |
| Dynamic group links | Lets people combine usernames in one URL, for example `cal.com/anna+brian`, to book a meeting with everyone. See [Dynamic group links](/event-types/dynamic). |
| Allow search engine indexing | Lets search engines show your public booking pages in search results. |
| Monthly digest email | Sends you one monthly summary of booking activity across every team you administer. |
| Prevent impersonation on bookings | Anyone who books using your email address must verify they own it first. |
