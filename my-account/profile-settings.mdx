---
title: "Profile settings"
description: "Change your profile picture, username, name, email addresses and public bio, or delete your account."
---

Go to **Settings** > **My account** > [**Profile**](https://app.cal.com/settings/my-account/profile). What you enter here appears on your public booking page.

## Profile details

- **Profile picture**: click **Upload avatar** to add or change it, or **Remove** to clear it.
- **Username**: your booking page URL, for example `cal.com/yourname`. Changing it changes all your booking links, so update any links you've already shared.
- **Full name**: the name bookers see.
- **About**: a short bio for your booking page.
- **Location**: a free-text location, for example "London, UK".
- **Social links**: click **Add social link** to show links to your other profiles.

Click **Update** to save.

## Email addresses

Your **Primary** email is where Cal.com sends booking emails and the address you sign in with.

- Click **Add email** to add another address. Cal.com sends a verification link, and the address is marked **Unverified** until you click it.
- Open the menu next to an address to **Make primary**, **Resend email** or **Delete** it.

When you change your primary email, Cal.com sends a verification link to the new address and only switches once you confirm it. You may be asked for your password.

## Connected accounts

If you signed in with Google or another identity provider, it is listed under **Connected accounts**. Click **Disconnect** to remove it. If you haven't set a password yet, you're asked to create one first so you can still sign in.

## Delete your account

Under **Danger zone**, click **Delete account**, enter your password and click **Delete my account**. This can't be undone: your booking links stop working and your data is removed.
