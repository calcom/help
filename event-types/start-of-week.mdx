---
title: "Start of week for a booking link"
description: "Choose which day the booking calendar starts on for a single booking link."
---

By default, the calendar on your booking pages starts the week on the day set in your account settings. You can change this for one booking link without affecting your other links.

1. Open the booking link and go to **Appearance**.
2. Under **Start of week**, choose the day you want the calendar to start on.
3. Click **Save**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/event-types/start-of-week.png" alt="The Start of week setting on a booking link's Appearance tab" />
</Frame>

Choose **Account default** to go back to using your account setting. The preview next to the settings shows how the calendar will look to bookers.
