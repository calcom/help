---
title: "Cal Video rooms and Cal Audio"
description: "Start meetings that don't need a booking, use your team's permanent room, and host voice-only rooms on Cal Audio."
---

Most Cal Video meetings are created for you when someone books a Link. You can also open a room directly, without a booking. This is useful for quick calls, a standing team meeting, or a voice-only chat.

Only the host needs a Cal.com account. Guests can join any room from the link, without signing up.

---

## Start a one-off meeting

Go to [cal.video](https://cal.video), sign in, and click **Start a one-off meeting**. Cal Video creates a room with a short address made of three words. Share the link with the people you want to meet.

## Your personal room

On a paid plan, you also get a personal room at `cal.video/<your-username>`. The address never changes, so you can share it once and reuse it for every call. If you're in an organization, your personal room is on your organization's address, for example `acme.cal.video/<your-username>`.

---

## Permanent team rooms

Every team in an organization has its own permanent room at `<organization>.cal.video/<team-slug>`. For example, the Sales team at Acme meets at `acme.cal.video/sales`.

- The room belongs to the team, not to one person, and it doesn't expire.
- It's created the first time a team member opens the link while signed in. After that, anyone with the link sees the join screen.
- Guests knock and wait for a host to let them in, unless a host has opened the room to anyone with the link.
- The room's features follow the team's plan.
- Recording isn't available in team rooms.

<Note>
  Team rooms are only available for organizations. Teams outside an organization don't have a permanent room.

## Cal Audio

[Cal Audio](https://cal.audio) works like Cal Video, but rooms are voice only. Use it for calls where you just need to talk, such as a quick check-in or a podcast-style conversation.

- There's no camera. People appear with their profile picture or initials.
- You can still share your screen.
- There's no waiting screen. Opening the link puts you straight into the room, under your Cal.com name or a generated name such as "Quiet Otter". You can change your name on your own tile.
- If the host requires approval, guests knock and wait to be let in, the same as on Cal Video.
- Recording and captions aren't available.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/cal-video/cal-audio-home.png" alt="Cal Audio home page showing an example room address" />
</Frame>

To start a room, go to [cal.audio](https://cal.audio), sign in, and click **Start a one-off room**. Organizations get their own address, such as `acme.cal.audio`.

<Tip>
  `cal.audio/<room>` and `cal.video/<room>` are separate rooms, even when the names match. Share the right link so everyone ends up in the same place.
</Tip>

If your browser blocks sound when you join, Cal Audio shows a notice. Click it to hear the room.
