Cal Video rooms don't need a booking. You can keep a permanent link for yourself or your team, and use cal.audio for calls that are voice only.

## Your personal room

Your personal room is at `cal.video/<your-username>`. In an organization it's at `<org>.cal.video/<your-username>`. It's created the first time you open it while signed in, and it's available on paid plans.

## Permanent team rooms

In an organization, every team has a permanent room at `<org>.cal.video/<team-slug>`. For example, the team at `acme.cal.com/engineering` meets at `acme.cal.video/engineering`.

- The room is created the first time a member of the team opens it while signed in. After that it never expires.
- Every member of the team is a host of the room.
- Recording isn't available in team rooms.
- Team rooms only exist in organizations.

## Who can get in

A room is closed until one of its hosts opens it. Until then, anyone else who opens the link sees the same page as for a room that doesn't exist.

While the meeting is running, guests knock and wait for a host to let them in. A host can open the room to anyone with the link from the host controls in the call toolbar. The room stays open for future meetings until a host switches it back. People with an invite link from a host can join directly either way.

## Cal Audio

cal.audio has the same kind of rooms with no video, for calls where you only need to talk.

- There's no pre-join screen. Open the link and you're in the call.
- If you're signed in, you join under your Cal.com name. Otherwise you get a generated name, which you can change by editing the name on your own tile.
- Everyone is shown as a round avatar, and it lights up while that person is speaking.
- Screen sharing works. Cameras can't be turned on, and recording and captions aren't available yet.
- Organizations get their own rooms at `<org>.cal.audio`.
- `cal.audio/<room>` and `cal.video/<room>` are two different rooms, even if the name is the same.
- cal.audio has its own sign-in, so being signed in on cal.video doesn't sign you in on cal.audio.

If you can't hear anything, your browser may be blocking sound until you interact with the page. The room tells you when this happens and gives you a button to try again. If your microphone is blocked or missing, you can still listen, and the room lets you know nobody can hear you.
