---
title: Offer seats
description: "Let more than one attendee book the same time slot, and review seat requests one by one when the link requires confirmation."
---

When **Offer seats** is turned on for a booking link, more than one attendee can book the same time slot. This is useful for classes, seminars, webinars, and any other session that has a fixed number of attendance spots.

## Turn on seats

1. Open the booking link and go to **Payments & Seats**.
2. Turn on **Offer seats**.
3. Enter the **Number of seats per booking**.
4. Click **Save**.

Attendees can keep booking the slot until every seat is taken.

<Note>
  A seated booking has one shared location for everyone, so seats can't be combined with the **In Person (Attendee's Address)** location. That location uses a single attendee's address, which would send every other attendee to that person's address. While the link uses that location, the **Offer seats** toggle is unavailable and the app asks you to pick another location. Choose a different location first, such as an in-person address you set, a phone number, or a video call.
</Note>

Booking links that already combined seats with **In Person (Attendee's Address)** before this restriction can still be saved, but you can't newly add that combination.

## Review seat requests

If a seated booking link also has **Requires confirmation** turned on, each attendee who books a seat is added as a request. You decide on each seat individually, without confirming or rejecting the whole session.

To review seat requests:

1. Go to **Bookings** and open the session. Seat requests show up on the **Unconfirmed** tab until you accept someone.
2. In the booking details, the seat count shows how many seats are taken. Attendees who are waiting for a decision are listed under **Waiting**.
3. Click **Accept** next to an attendee, or select several attendees and use **Accept** or **Reject** at the bottom of the panel.

Accepted attendees move to **In the room**. You can still reject someone there later, which frees up their seat. Rejected attendees are removed from the list.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/seats/seat-requests-in-the-room.png" alt="An accepted attendee listed under In the room, with the remaining requests under Waiting" />
</Frame>

A few things to know:

- You can't accept more people than the seats that are left. When the session is full, reject someone to free a seat.
- Use **Search by name or email** to find a specific attendee in a long list.
- Once you accept the first attendee, the session shows as confirmed. Anyone you haven't decided on stays under **Waiting**.
- The seat list is only shown to people who can confirm bookings for this link, and only until the session has ended.

Seated booking links that don't require confirmation show the regular attendee list instead. Booking links that require confirmation but don't offer seats keep the usual **Confirm** and **Reject** actions for the whole booking.
