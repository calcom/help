Cal Pay is Cal.com's own payment option. It is available on every account by default, so there is nothing to install from the app store and no Stripe or PayPal account to connect before you can charge for a booking.

Payments are processed by Whop. Cal.com does not take a commission on what you charge.

## Turn on Cal Pay for a Link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Link you want to charge for">
    Go to your Links and open the one you want to charge for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Payments & Seats">
    In the left menu of the Link, under **Booking experience**, select **Payments & Seats**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Paid booking">
    Switch on **Paid booking**, then choose **Cal Pay** under **Select a payment app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a price and currency">
    Enter the **Price** and choose the **Currency**. The price must be at least the currency's minimum charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a refund policy">
    Pick how refunds are handled: **Always**, **Never**, or **If cancelled** a set number of days or business days before the booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. Every new booking on that Link now requires payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the booker sees

The booker fills in your booking form as usual and pays before the booking is created. Checkout runs inside the booking page itself, so they are never redirected to an external payment page. Once the payment goes through, the booking is confirmed and they receive their confirmation and a receipt.

Bookers can pay by card, ACH, bank wire, Cash App, or crypto.

## Balance and withdrawals

Go to **Apps** > **Installed apps** > **Payment**. Under **Cal Pay** you will find **Balance and withdrawals**, where you can see your **Total balance** and **Available balance**, make a withdrawal, and complete payout verification without leaving Cal.com.

Identity verification (KYC) is required before any money can reach you, but only at the point of your first withdrawal, not when you set Cal Pay up. Complete it before you are expecting your first payout.

## Cal Pay and Stripe

Both options exist for Links. If you already collect payments through the [Stripe app](/event-types/how-to-receive-payments), that keeps working. Cal Pay is the option that needs no external account.

For [events](/events/paid-events), Cal Pay is the only option. Stripe and PayPal cannot be used to sell event tickets.
