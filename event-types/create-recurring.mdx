---
title: "Create a recurring link"
---

Recurring links let bookers subscribe to a series of appointments, such as a weekly class or a monthly meeting.

## Set up a recurring link

1. Open **Links** and select the booking link you want to edit.
2. Choose **Recurring**.
3. Turn on **Recurring event**.
4. Under **Repeats every**, enter an interval from 1 to 20 and choose a weekly, monthly, or yearly schedule.
5. Under **For a maximum of**, enter the number of events in the series. A series can have up to 32 occurrences. Bookers can choose fewer occurrences, up to this maximum.
6. Select **Save**.

For example, a yoga instructor can offer a weekly class for up to ten sessions.

Recurring events are not available with seats, payments, or the per-booker booking limit.
