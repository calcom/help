Add a banner image to a Link to brand its booking page, for example with your logo, a product image or a campaign visual.

---

## Add a banner to a Link

<Note>
  Banners are available on Links in an organization.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Link">
    Go to **Links**, open the Link and select **Appearance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the banner">
    Under **Banner**, click **Upload banner** and choose an image. Crop it to fit. The recommended size is 1500 × 500 pixels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The banner appears at the top of the Link's booking page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/booking-page-banner/link-banner-setting.png" alt="Appearance tab of a Link with the Banner section and an uploaded banner" />
</Frame>

A Link's banner replaces the organization banner on that booking page. On the booking page, the banner is shown at a 3:1 ratio, so keep important parts of the image away from the edges.

Images can be up to 5 MB. If an image is too large after cropping, you'll be asked to use a smaller one. JPEG files usually work best.
