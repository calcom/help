Switching to Cal.com from Calendly or SavvyCal? You can copy your setup and booking history over in a few steps.

## Start an import

1. Go to **Settings > My account > Import**.
2. On the Calendly or SavvyCal card, click **Import from Calendly** or **Import from SavvyCal**.
3. Sign in to that account and allow Cal.com to read it.
4. Review what was found and choose what to bring over.
5. Start the import. It runs in the background, and you get an email when it's done.

</Frame>

If you decline access on the Calendly or SavvyCal screen, you're sent back to the Import page and nothing is imported.

## Choose what to bring over

The review goes through each type of item in turn: availability schedules, booking links, routing forms, workflows and bookings, then a final confirmation. Steps with nothing to import are skipped.

- Everything starts selected. Untick anything you don't want, or use select all.
- For bookings, you can import all of them, only upcoming ones, or none. Bookings on a booking link you unticked aren't imported.
- Finish the review within two hours of connecting. After that, the review expires and you need to connect again from the Import page.

## What gets imported

**From Calendly**

| Imported | Not imported |
| --- | --- |
| Availability schedules | Reminder workflows |
| Booking links, with their booking questions and locations | Polls |
| Team booking links, hidden until you assign hosts | Contacts |
| Routing forms, questions only | Team members |
| Booking history, with payments and UTM tracking | Connected conferencing accounts |
| | Meeting notes and transcripts |
| | Single-use scheduling links |
| | Webhook subscriptions |

Routing forms come over with their questions, but you need to rebuild the routing rules yourself.

**From SavvyCal**

| Imported | Not imported |
| --- | --- |
| Booking links, with their booking questions | Availability schedules |
| Team booking links, hidden until you assign hosts | Locations |
| Reminder workflows | Polls |
| Booking history, with payments | Team members |
| | Workflow automations (webhooks) |
| | Webhook subscriptions |

SavvyCal email reminders become Cal.com [workflows](/workflows/workflowsoverview) with an email step.

## When the import is done

The email lists the booking links, schedules and bookings that were created, and how many items were skipped. Check your imported booking links before you share them. Team booking links stay hidden until you assign hosts.
