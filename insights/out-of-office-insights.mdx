The **Out of office** tab in [Insights](https://app.cal.com/insights/out-of-office) reports on the [out-of-office](/availabilities/out-of-office) entries your team members create.

Open it from **Insights** in the left sidebar, then select **Out of office**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/insights/out-of-office.png" alt="Out of office reasons over time chart" />
</Frame>

## Filters

- **Team**: choose a team, or the whole organization.
- **Date range**: choose a preset or a custom range. The timezone badge next to it shows which timezone the dates use.

## Charts and tables

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Out of office reasons over time | How many out-of-office days were logged per reason (Vacation, Travel, Sick leave, Public holiday, Unspecified) in each period |
| Reason breakdown | One chart per reason, so you can follow a single reason on its own |
| Average sick days per member over time | Total sick-leave days in each period divided by the number of team members |
| Recent out of office | The latest out-of-office entries in the selected range, including who bookings were forwarded to |
| Most out of office | Members with the most out-of-office days in the range |
| Least out of office | Members with the fewest out-of-office days. Only members with at least one entry are listed |

<Note>
  Team-wide out-of-office data is only visible to team admins and owners.
</Note>
