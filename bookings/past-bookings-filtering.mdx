---
title: "Filter bookings by date"
description: "Filter your bookings list by when bookings take place or by when they were created."
---

The **Past** tab in Bookings shows all of your past bookings. To narrow the list down, add a date filter from the **Filter** button at the top of the bookings list. There are two date filters:

- **Date range** filters by when the booking takes place.
- **Created at** filters by when the booking was made, no matter when it's scheduled.

For example, use **Created at** to find every booking that came in last week, including bookings scheduled for next month.

## Add a date filter

1. Go to **Bookings** and pick a tab, such as **Past**.
2. Click **Filter** and choose **Date range** or **Created at**.
3. Choose one of the preset options, or pick your own start and end dates on the calendar:
   - **Today**
   - **Last 7 days**
   - **Last 30 days**
   - **Last 90 days**
   - **Month to date**
   - **Year to date**
   - **Custom range**

The filter appears as a pill at the top of the list, and the list updates to show the matching bookings. You can use both filters at the same time.

## How the date filters work

- **Created at** only offers past dates, because a booking can't have been created in the future. It's available on every bookings tab.
- **Date range** only offers dates that fit the tab you're on: past dates on **Past**, future dates on **Upcoming**, **Unconfirmed**, and **Recurring**, and any date on **Cancelled**.
- Both filters include the whole first and last day you select.

## Remove or save filters

Click the **x** on a filter pill to remove it, or click **Clear** to remove all filters. To reuse a set of filters later, click **Save** and pick it from **Saved filters** next time.
