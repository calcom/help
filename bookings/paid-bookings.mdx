Paid bookings let you charge for a booking with [Cal Pay](/cal-pay), Cal.com's built-in payment option, or with Stripe. With Stripe you can also charge a no-show fee later instead of collecting payment up front.

## Set up payments for a booking link

1. Open **Links** and select the booking link.
2. Open **Payments & Seats** and turn on **Paid booking**.
3. Under **Select a payment app**, choose **Cal Pay** or **Stripe**. For Cal Pay, follow [Cal Pay](/cal-pay) from here. The rest of these steps cover Stripe. If Stripe is not installed, select it under **Connect payment apps** and complete setup.
4. Enter the **Price** and choose the **Currency**.
5. Under **Payment option**, choose **Collect payment on booking** or **Charge no-show fee**.
6. Set the **Refund policy** to **Always**, **Never**, or **If cancelled**, then choose the number of business or calendar days before the event.
7. To charge for last-minute cancellations, turn on **Auto charge no-show fee for last minute cancellations** and set how many minutes, hours, or days before the scheduled start time to charge.
8. Select **Save**.

With a no-show fee, the booker's card is saved and charged later. For a booking, use **Charge card** or **Collect no-show fee** to collect it. No-show fees cannot be used with seats, and paid bookings are not compatible with recurring events.

For setup details, see [how to receive payments](/event-types/how-to-receive-payments). To collect a no-show fee for a booking, see [manage a booking](/bookings/manage-a-booking).
