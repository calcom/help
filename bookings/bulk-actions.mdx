---
title: "Bulk actions on bookings"
description: "Select several bookings at once to accept, reject, cancel, reschedule or delete them."
---

When you need to handle many bookings at once, you don't have to open them one by one. Select them on the **Bookings** page and apply one action to all of them.

---

## Select bookings

1. Go to **Bookings**.
2. Tick the checkbox next to each booking you want to change. A bar appears that shows how many bookings are selected.
3. Choose an action from the bar.

You can select up to 100 bookings at a time.

## Available actions

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Accept bookings** | Confirms the selected bookings that are waiting for your approval. Guests get the usual confirmation. |
| **Reject bookings** | Declines the selected bookings that are waiting for your approval. You can add one reason, which is sent to every guest. |
| **Request reschedule** | Cancels the selected bookings, notifies the guests and asks them to pick a new time. You can add one reason for all of them. |
| **Cancel bookings** | Cancels the selected bookings. You can add one cancellation reason, which is sent to every guest. |
| **Delete bookings** | Permanently removes the selected bookings. Guests are **not** notified, and this can't be undone. |

<Warning>
  Deleting a booking doesn't tell the guest anything. If guests should know, cancel the bookings or request a reschedule first.
</Warning>

---

## Things to know

- Bulk actions run in the background. Large selections can take a moment to finish.
- Each booking is checked on its own. If some bookings can't take the action, for example a booking that already ended or isn't waiting for approval, those are skipped and the rest are updated.
- **Accept** and **Reject** only change bookings that are waiting for approval. You'll find those on the **Unconfirmed** tab.
- Reasons for rejecting or cancelling can be up to 500 characters.
- To mark a guest as a no-show, open the booking and use **Mark as no-show**. This isn't a bulk action.
- On team and organization bookings, you can only change bookings you have permission to manage.
