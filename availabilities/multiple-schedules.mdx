---
title: "Multiple schedules"
---

You can set multiple schedules in the availability section of the application and organize your booking links better by customizing your availability, quite simply, by selecting respective schedule as the availability for an individual booking link. You can check [Setting up your availability](/availabilities/availability-dashboard-overview) to link your created availability schedule with a booking link.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/i1600x899-oXaqBFnOdR-p_kvdzt6.png" alt="Multiple schedules interface" />
</Frame>

This feature is particularly useful if you wish to dedicate different booking links over different time-zones as you can set up an availability schedule for every time-zone you wish to schedule around.
