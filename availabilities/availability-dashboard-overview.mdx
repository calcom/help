---
title: "Set up your availability"
description: "Create availability schedules, set your weekly hours, add date overrides and pick the timezone people book you in."
sidebarTitle: 'Overview'
---

Your availability decides when people can book you. You can keep one schedule for everything or create several, for example one for client calls and one for internal meetings, and pick which schedule each booking link uses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your availability">
    Log in to Cal.com and click **Availability** in the sidebar. You'll see your existing schedules, such as **Working hours**, with the days, hours and timezone each one uses. The schedule marked **Default** is used by any booking link that doesn't have its own schedule.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/availability/availability-page.png" alt="Availability page with the Availability item in the sidebar and the Working hours schedule highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new schedule">
    Click **New** in the top-right corner, enter a name for the schedule (for example "Test Availability") and click **Continue**. New schedules start as Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/availability/new-schedule.png" alt="Add a new schedule dialog with the Name field and the Continue button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your weekly hours">
    In **Weekly hours**, choose when you're available each day:

    - **Turn days on or off:** use the toggle next to a day. Days that are off show as **Unavailable**.
    - **Change the hours:** click a start or end time and pick a new one. For example, turn on Saturday and set it to 9:00am to 1:00pm.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/availability/weekly-hours.png" alt="Weekly hours card with the Saturday toggle turned on and its end time set to 1:00pm" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more than one time slot to a day">
    To split a day into separate blocks, click the **+** button on that day's row. A new time range is added below the first one, and you can set its hours the same way. For example, you can be available 9:00am to 11:00am and again 5:00pm to 7:30pm. Click the **X** next to a range to remove it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/availability/add-time-slot.png" alt="Monday row with a second time range added using the plus button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy hours to other days">
    Once one day looks right, click the copy button next to it. In **Copy times to**, select the days you want to match (or **Select all**) and click **Apply**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/availability/copy-times.png" alt="Copy times to menu open from Tuesday's copy button with the list of days and the Apply button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add date overrides">
    Use a date override when your hours are different on specific dates, such as a holiday or a day you're finishing early. Click **Add an override** in the **Date overrides** section, then:

    - Pick one or more dates in the calendar.
    - Under **Which hours are you free?**, set the hours for those dates, or turn on **Mark unavailable (All day)**.
    - Click **Save override**.

    Overrides are archived automatically once the date has passed.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/availability/date-override.png" alt="Date override dialog with a calendar to pick dates and the hours and Mark unavailable options" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the timezone">
    Use the **Timezone** menu to set the timezone for this schedule. This is useful if you travel and want a schedule that follows another country's time.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/availability/timezone.png" alt="Timezone menu at the top right of the schedule page" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your schedule">
    Click **Save** in the top-right corner to apply your changes. To use this schedule for every booking link that doesn't have its own schedule, turn on **Set as default**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Troubleshoot your availability">
    If times aren't showing up the way you expect, click **Launch troubleshooter** under **Something doesn't look right?**. It shows how your schedule, calendar events and other settings combine to decide when you can be booked. See [Troubleshoot your availability](/availabilities/troubleshoot-your-availability) for examples.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/availability/troubleshooter.png" alt="Something doesn't look right card with the Launch troubleshooter button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
