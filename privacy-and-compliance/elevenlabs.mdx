## What ElevenLabs does

ElevenLabs is a Cal.com subprocessor that turns speech into text, and text into speech for Cal Notes. Cal Video and Cal Notes use it for:

- **Live captions** during a call.
- **Transcripts** of recorded calls, after the call.
- **Cal Notes** speech on the desktop app.

Meeting audio and the resulting transcripts are processed by ElevenLabs in the United States.

### What is sent

- The audio of people speaking while captions or a transcript are on, not only the host.
- The transcripts made from that audio.
- In Cal Notes, the text you ask it to read aloud.
- In Cal Notes, attendee names and words from the meeting title, to help recognise them.
- In Cal Notes, your custom vocabulary (dictionary entries), to help recognise them.

## Your account approves once

Before any audio goes to ElevenLabs, someone allowed to decide for your account ticks **“I’m allowed to agree for this account”**:

- For an **organization**, an organization admin.
- For a **team outside an organization**, a team admin.
- For **your own account**, you.

One approval covers captions, transcripts and Cal Notes for the account. Cal Notes on a personal account needs its own approval.

A team inside an organization follows the organization's approval. Approving for a team outside an organization covers that team's calls; it doesn't approve a member's personal account.

Until the account approves, nothing is sent to ElevenLabs: captions don’t start, automatic captions stay off, recorded calls aren’t transcribed by ElevenLabs, and Cal Notes speech is unavailable.

### Turn approval on or off in settings

Organization admins can open [organization privacy settings](https://app.cal.com/settings/organizations/privacy). Admins of a team outside an organization can open **Settings**, select their team, then open **Settings** (`/settings/teams/<team-id>/settings`). Teams inside an organization use the organization's privacy setting.

Find **Third-party transcription and captions**, just below **Make organization private** or **Make team private**.

- **Turn it on:** read the approval dialog, including the **Details** and **Subprocessors** links. Check the account name, tick **"I'm allowed to agree for this account"**, then select **Approve**. Opening the dialog or cancelling it gives no approval.
- **Turn it off:** confirm **Turn off** to withdraw the account's approval for captions, recording transcripts and Cal Notes speech. Turning it back on requires approval again.

Admins can also approve when they first turn on captions in a call. People using their own account keep approving in the call or in Cal Notes; they don't get an organization or team settings toggle.

### The sidebar reminder

Admins of an organization or a team outside an organization may see **Action required**, **Turn on transcription and captions**, and **So your team can keep using them in Cal Video and Cal Notes.** in the expanded desktop sidebar. The reminder appears for accounts that still need approval. It is hidden when the sidebar is collapsed and on smaller screens. Personal accounts don't get this reminder.

- **Review** opens the approval dialog. The dialog names the accounts being approved and shows the disclosure, links and authorization checkbox. Selecting Review alone gives no approval.
- **Dismiss** permanently hides the reminder for those accounts, including for other admins and in other browsers. It doesn't approve or withdraw anything. An admin can still turn approval on later in the account's settings.

### Notify your admin

Signed-in members who can start captions but can't approve for the account can select **Notify your admin** in the captions dialog. Hosts who meet the same conditions also see this option when automatic captions are held back because their account hasn't approved ElevenLabs. People who can approve get the approval option instead.

The email asks the account's admins to review ElevenLabs in the organization's privacy settings or the standalone team's settings. Notifications are limited to once every 24 hours per account, shared across everyone who asks. After a notification has been sent, the button reads **Admin notified**. Sending the email doesn't approve ElevenLabs.

<h2 id="who-this-approval-covers">Who this approval covers</h2>

Approving in the product covers accounts on Cal.com’s standard online DPA. Signed a separate Cal.com DPA before November 2026, with no amendment? This approval doesn’t apply. Contact [privacy@cal.com](mailto:privacy@cal.com).

Don’t send protected health information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA. ElevenLabs doesn’t allow it without a HIPAA business associate agreement, and Cal.com doesn’t have one with ElevenLabs.

## Retention and model training

ElevenLabs processes audio and transcripts for Cal.com under a data processing agreement. Cal.com has opted out of ElevenLabs using them to train or improve its models. ElevenLabs keeps them only as long as it needs to provide, troubleshoot and secure its service; it doesn’t publish a fixed period. Transcripts of booked calls stay in Cal.com until you delete them. Transcripts from rooms without a booking are deleted after 30 days.

## Participants

On Cal Video calls using RealtimeKit and ElevenLabs, guests outside the account that owns the call see an unticked checkbox before joining, once every account responsible for the call has approved ElevenLabs. This applies to calls owned by an organization, a team outside an organization, or a personal account. Members covered by all of those accounts don't see it. Daily calls don't show this checkbox.

The checkbox says the call uses captions and transcription when captions are already running or a recording will be transcribed. Otherwise, it says the host can turn them on. Both versions name ElevenLabs and include **Details** and **Privacy policy** links. Guests must tick it to join. They are asked on every call, including when they leave and rejoin; a previous tick isn't carried over.

If a guest joined before the account approved, approving during that call saves the account's approval for future calls. Captions and recording transcripts stay off for that call because the guest wasn't asked before joining.

While captions are on, everyone in the call sees an indicator.

Your organization remains responsible for any notice or consent your own policies or local law require.

## Stopping processing

- **During a call:** the host can turn captions off at any time.
- **Automatic captions:** turn off automatic transcription in the event type’s Cal Video settings.
- **Cal Notes:** turn off cloud meeting transcription in the desktop app, and don’t use briefing Listen or read-aloud.
- **Withdrawing an organization's or standalone team's approval:** turn off **Third-party transcription and captions** in the account's settings and confirm. This withdraws approval for every covered feature, including Cal Notes speech. For a personal account, contact [privacy@cal.com](mailto:privacy@cal.com).

Withdrawing approval blocks new speech-processing requests. In a call already using captions, an active stream ends at its next authorization check. It doesn’t delete what was already processed: transcripts saved to your account follow the retention periods above, and ElevenLabs keeps its copy under its own retention terms.

## What we record

For each approval we keep the account, the approving user’s ID and role, the time, the exact wording version they saw, and where they approved (captions, Cal Notes, settings or the sidebar reminder). We store IDs, not names or email addresses. Records are kept while the account exists and for six years after it is deleted, then removed automatically.

The wording people approve is versioned and published versions are kept for six years. A material change, such as different data, retention, location or exclusions, asks every account to approve again. Wording fixes don’t.
