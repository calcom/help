## What ElevenLabs does

ElevenLabs is a Cal.com subprocessor that turns speech into text. Cal Video uses it for:

- **Live captions** during a call.
- **Transcripts** of recorded calls, after the call.
- **Cal Notes** speech on the desktop app.

Meeting audio and the resulting transcripts are processed by ElevenLabs in the United States.

### What is sent

- The audio of everyone speaking while captions or a transcript are on, not only the host.
- The transcripts made from that audio.
- In Cal Notes, the text you ask it to read aloud.

## Your account approves once

Before any audio goes to ElevenLabs, someone allowed to decide for your account ticks **“I’m allowed to agree for this account”**:

- For an **organization**, an organization admin.
- For a **team outside an organization**, a team admin.
- For **your own account**, you.

One approval covers captions, transcripts and Cal Notes for everyone in the account. Nobody in the account is asked again.

Admins can approve when they first turn on captions in a call, from the banner in the Cal.com web app, or when they turn on cloud speech in Cal Notes. Until the account approves, nothing is sent to ElevenLabs: captions don’t start, automatic captions stay off, recorded calls aren’t transcribed, and Cal Notes speech is unavailable. Members who press Captions are asked to contact their admin.

<h2 id="who-this-approval-covers">Who this approval covers</h2>

Approving in the product covers accounts on Cal.com’s standard online DPA. It doesn’t cover organizations that signed Cal.com’s legacy DPA, which requires your prior written authorization for new subprocessors and transfers outside the EEA. If this applies to you, don’t use the in-product approval. Email [privacy@cal.com](mailto:privacy@cal.com) and we’ll send you a written authorization, or the DPA amendment, to sign.

## Participants

Everyone joining a Cal Video call sees, before they join, that the host may use transcriptions and captions processed by ElevenLabs, with a link to this page. While captions are on, everyone in the call can see it.

Your organization remains responsible for any notice your own policies or local law require.

## Stopping processing

- **During a call:** the host can turn captions off at any time.
- **Automatic captions:** turn off automatic transcription in the event type’s Cal Video settings.
- **Cal Notes:** turn off cloud speech in the desktop app.
- **Withdrawing your account’s approval:** email [privacy@cal.com](mailto:privacy@cal.com).

Stopping processing stops new audio from being sent. It doesn’t delete what was already processed; see [Retention and model training](#retention-and-model-training).

## What we record

For each approval we keep the account, the approving user’s ID and role, the time, the exact wording version they saw, and where they approved (captions, Cal Notes or the banner). We store IDs, not names or email addresses. Records are kept while the account exists and for six years after it is deleted, then removed automatically.

The wording people approve is versioned and published versions are kept for six years. A material change, such as different data, retention, location or exclusions, asks every account to approve again. Wording fixes don’t.
