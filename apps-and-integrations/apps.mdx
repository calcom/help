title: "Apps"
---

Apps connect Cal.com to external services or add features to your account.

## App store

Open **Apps > App store** to browse apps. Browse **Featured categories** and sections such as **Most popular** and **Recently added**, or use **Search** to find an app by name. App cards show a name and description, with a **Details** button.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/apps/app-store.png" alt="The App store showing featured categories, search, and app cards." />
</Frame>

Select **Details** to review an app's category, publisher, description, pricing, and contact information. For apps that support installation in Cal.com, select **Install app** and follow the setup prompts. Some apps provide an external link instead.

## Installed apps

Open **Apps > Installed apps** to manage the apps on your account. The page URL follows `/apps/installed/<category>`. The category tabs are:

- **Analytics**
- **AI & Automation**
- **Calendar**
- **Conferencing**
- **CRM**
- **Messaging**
- **Payment**
- **Other**

In a category, select **Add** to add an app. In Conferencing, the **Default** label marks the conferencing app currently set as default. To remove an installed app, open its options menu and select **Remove app**.
