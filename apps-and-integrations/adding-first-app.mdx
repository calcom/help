---
title: "Adding your first app"
---

Open **Apps > App store** to browse apps. Browse a featured category or use **Search** to find an app by name.

1. Find an app card and select **Details**.
2. Review the app details, then select **Install app**.
3. Follow the prompts to complete setup or authorize the app if requested.
4. After setup, manage the app under **Apps > Installed apps**.
