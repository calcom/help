---
title: "How to use Cal.com with Attio"
---

The Attio integration (Cal Sync) syncs your Cal.com bookings and meetings into Attio, so your CRM stays up to date with your scheduling activity. You install it from Attio.

## Before you start

You need three things:

1. An account on both Cal.com and Attio
2. Google Calendar connected to Attio
3. Google Calendar connected to Cal.com

<Note>
  The Google Calendar account you connect must use the same email domain as your Attio account. For example, if you sign in to Attio as jane@acme.com, connect a Google Calendar on acme.com.
</Note>

## Connect Cal.com to Attio

1. In Cal.com, go to **Apps**, search for **Attio** and click **Visit**. This opens the [Cal Sync app page on Attio](https://attio.com/apps/cal-sync).
2. Follow the steps on Attio to connect your Cal.com account.
