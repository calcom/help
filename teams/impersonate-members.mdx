Impersonation lets someone with the required permission temporarily sign in as another member to help with their Cal.com setup or troubleshoot an issue. You don't need the member's password.

<Warning>
  This is not a read-only preview. While impersonating someone, you can make changes on their behalf. Check the username in the impersonation banner before making changes, and stop impersonating when you're finished.
</Warning>

## Who can impersonate members?

| Context | Who can do it by default | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| Standalone team | Owners and admins of that team | The team's **Members** page |
| Organization | Organization owners and admins | The organization's **Members** page, or a team's **Members** page where you have access and impersonation permission |

- Members don't have impersonation permission by default. If your organization uses custom roles or has adjusted team roles, access depends on the permissions assigned to your role. See [Roles and permissions](/roles/roles-and-permissions).
- An admin cannot impersonate an owner. You cannot impersonate yourself.
- The person you're impersonating must have accepted their invitation and must allow impersonation.
- Being an admin of one standalone team does not give you access to people in another team.

## Impersonate a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team's member list">
    Go to **Settings**, find your team under **My teams**, and select **Members**. You can also open the menu on the [Teams](https://app.cal.com/teams) page, choose **Edit team**, and then select **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the member">
    Open the **⋯** menu next to the member you want to help and select **Impersonate**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/impersonation/team-member-menu.png" alt="Team Members page with the member's three-dot menu open and Impersonate highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm impersonation">
    Read the warning, then click **Impersonate** in the confirmation dialog. Cal.com switches you into the member's account.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/impersonation/confirm-impersonation.png" alt="Confirmation dialog warning that impersonation allows changes on the member's behalf, with the Impersonate button highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Impersonate an organization member

You can start from either the organization's member list or a team's member list. Both sign you into the member's account, not a read-only view limited to that team.

### From the organization's Members page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organization members">
    Go to **Settings**, find your organization's name in the sidebar, and select **Members**. You can also open [Members](https://app.cal.com/members) from the main sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the member">
    Find the person, open their **⋯** menu, and select **Impersonate**. You don't need to find which team they belong to first.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/impersonation/organization-member-menu.png" alt="Organization Members page with Impersonate highlighted in a member's three-dot menu" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm impersonation">
    Click **Impersonate** in the confirmation dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### From a team within the organization

Open that team's **Members** page under **Settings**, then use the member's **⋯** menu > **Impersonate** and confirm, just as you would for a standalone team.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/impersonation/organization-team-member-menu.png" alt="Members page for a team within an organization, with Impersonate highlighted in the member's menu" />
</Frame>

Opening a team's settings does not grant extra impersonation permissions. If you have organization-wide access but can't impersonate from a particular team's list, use the organization's **Members** page instead.

## Stop impersonating and return to your account

While impersonating, a banner at the top of Cal.com shows **Impersonating username** and the member's username. Click **Click here to stop** in that banner to return to your own account.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/impersonation/stop-impersonation.png" alt="Orange impersonation banner at the top of Cal.com with Click here to stop highlighted" />
</Frame>

For organizations, impersonation starts and stops are recorded in [Audit logs](/enterprise/audit-logs).

## Control who can impersonate you

### Turn off all impersonation

Go to **Settings** > **Security** > [**Impersonation**](https://app.cal.com/settings/security/impersonation) and turn off **User impersonation**. This blocks impersonation of your account by Cal.com support as well as team and organization owners and admins.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/impersonation/account-wide-impersonation.png" alt="Security Impersonation settings with the account-wide User impersonation switch highlighted" />
</Frame>

### Change your preference for one team

Go to **Settings** > your team > **Settings** and change **User impersonation**. This is your own preference for that team, not a switch that enables impersonation for everyone in the team.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/impersonation/team-impersonation-setting.png" alt="Team settings with the member's team-specific User impersonation preference highlighted" />
</Frame>

Turning this on does not override an account-wide opt-out in **Security** > **Impersonation**.

## Why is Impersonate missing or not working?

- **Check your permissions.** You need impersonation permission for the relevant team or organization. An admin cannot impersonate an owner.
- **Check the invitation.** Pending members must accept their invitation first.
- **Ask the member to check their preferences.** Their account-wide setting can block impersonation even when a team setting allows it.
- **Check the organization.** Organization impersonation requires Cal.com to have reviewed the organization. If the option is unavailable despite the correct permissions and member settings, contact Cal.com support.
