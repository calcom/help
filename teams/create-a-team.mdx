---
title: "Create a team"
description: "Create a team so members can share booking links, round-robin and collective events."
---

A team lets you share booking links between coworkers, such as [round-robin](/event-types/round-robin), [collective](/event-types/collective-events) and [managed](/event-types/managed-events) links, and gives the team its own public page.

## Create the team

1. Go to [**Teams**](https://app.cal.com/teams) in the left sidebar and click **New**.
2. Upload a **Team logo** (recommended size 64x64px, max 5 MB).
3. Enter a **Team name**. Cal.com suggests a **Team URL** from the name. You can edit it.
4. Add a **Team bio** (optional).
5. Click **Continue**.

If the team needs a paid plan, you are taken to checkout to finish creating it. Teams inside an [organization](/enterprise/members-onboarding) are covered by the organization's plan, so there is no checkout.

## Invite members

After the team is created, the **Invite** step lets you connect Google Workspace, invite people by email, upload a CSV file, or copy an invite link. You can also click **Skip for now** and invite people later. See [Invite team members](/teams/invite-team-members).

## Manage your teams

The [**Teams**](https://app.cal.com/teams) page lists every team you belong to. Open the menu on a team to:

- **Copy link to team** or **Preview team** to see its public page.
- **Edit team** to open its settings. See [Team settings](/teams/team-settings).
- **Invite team member**.
- **Publish team**, if the team has not been published yet.
- **Leave team**, or **Disband team** if you are the owner.

## Accept a team invite

When someone invites you to a team, the invite appears under **Pending invites** on the Teams page. Click **Accept** to join, or **Reject** to decline.
