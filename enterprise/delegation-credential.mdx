---
title: "Delegation Credential"
description: "Automatically connect calendars for every organization member using a single admin-managed credential."
---

Delegation Credential lets organization admins connect Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or on-premises Microsoft Exchange calendars for members on an email domain. Set up one credential per domain, using your primary domain or a [verified secondary domain](/enterprise/secondary-domains). You can use different providers for different domains.

---

## Why use Delegation Credential

Without Delegation Credential, every organization member must manually connect their calendar through OAuth. This creates friction during onboarding and means members who haven't completed setup won't have calendar integration.

With Delegation Credential:

- **Calendars are auto-connected** for eligible members whose email matches the configured domain.
- **No individual authorization required.** Members don't need to sign in to their calendar provider.
- **New members get instant calendar access** as soon as they're added to the organization
- **Admins maintain central control** over the calendar integration

---

## How it works

1. An organization admin creates a Delegation Credential for the domain and grants it access in the calendar provider.
2. Once enabled, Cal.com uses the credential to check eligible members' calendars for conflicts.
3. If a member hasn't already chosen a destination calendar, Cal.com uses their primary calendar for new bookings.

---

## Who can manage Delegation Credentials

- **Creating** a Delegation Credential — organization owners and admins only
- **Enabling** a Delegation Credential requires a verified email and an eligible owner or admin on the configured domain for the connection check.
- **Members** cannot disconnect a delegation-managed calendar credential, but they can still connect additional calendars manually

---

## What changes for members

Once a Delegation Credential is enabled:

- The matching calendar appears as connected without any action from the member. For on-premises Exchange, the member's primary email must also be verified.
- Members can select which calendars to check for conflicts during [onboarding](/enterprise/members-onboarding)
- Google Meet is also available as a booking location automatically when using Google Workspace
- Members cannot disconnect the delegation-managed credential

## Set up on-premises Microsoft Exchange

Use **Microsoft Exchange** for an on-premises server that supports Exchange Web Services (EWS). For Exchange Online, use the [Microsoft 365 setup](/enterprise/delegation-credential-microsoft-365) instead.

1. In your organization settings, open **Delegation credential** and select **Add delegation credential**.
2. Enter your verified domain and select **Microsoft Exchange**. Enter the public EWS URL, service account username and password, server version, and authentication method. Select **Create**.
3. In the Exchange Management Shell, follow the command shown in Cal.com to grant the service account the `ApplicationImpersonation` role. Scope the role to only the mailboxes Cal.com needs.
4. Turn on the credential. Cal.com checks the connection using an owner or admin mailbox on that domain. If the check fails, verify the endpoint, credentials, and impersonation access before trying again.

Exchange delegation connects calendars only. It doesn't add a video conferencing app.

## Disabling a Delegation Credential

Disabling a Delegation Credential:

- Immediately stops auto-connecting calendars for members who haven't manually connected their calendar
- Preserves existing calendar preferences (selected calendars and destination calendar)
- Background jobs clean up delegation-specific records over time

Members who need calendar access after disabling will need to [connect their calendar manually](/quick-start/calendar) through the Cal.com app store.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a member already connected their calendar manually?">
    The Delegation Credential takes priority for the matching domain. The member's manual connection is preserved but the delegation-managed credential is used for calendar operations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can members opt out of the Delegation Credential?">
    No. Members cannot disconnect a delegation-managed calendar credential. However, they can still connect additional calendars from other providers manually.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Delegation Credential available for individual users or teams?">
    No. Delegation Credential is an organization-level feature. Only organization owners and admins can create and manage them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
