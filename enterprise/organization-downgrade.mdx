---
title: "Downgrade an organization to Teams"
description: "Turn your organization into standalone Teams, choose what happens to each team, and review what will be removed."
---

If you no longer need an organization, you can downgrade it to the Teams plan. A step-by-step wizard shows the costs, lets you decide what happens to each team, and lists anything that will be lost before you confirm.

<Note>
  You need permission to manage the organization's billing. Organization owners have it by default.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Downgrading can't be undone from the wizard. Organization-only data, such as settings that only exist for organizations, is removed. Read the **Review** step carefully before you confirm.
</Warning>

---

## Start the downgrade

Open [Downgrade organization to Teams](https://app.cal.com/settings/organizations/downgrade) in your organization settings. The wizard has three steps: **Plan**, **Review** and **Confirm**. At the top, it compares your current organization cost with the estimated cost after the downgrade.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/organization-downgrade/downgrade-plan.png" alt="Downgrade organization to Teams wizard on the Plan step" />
</Frame>

### 1. Plan

For each team in your organization, choose whether it stays as a standalone team or closes and merges into one of the teams you're keeping (its **Merge destination**). The wizard shows each team's members, Links, routing forms and workflows to help you decide.

You need to keep at least one team. You also choose a **Credits destination**, the team that receives the organization's remaining credits.

### 2. Review

Check what stays, what moves and what will be removed. Two kinds of items can appear:

- **Warnings**: organization-only data that will be removed. You have to acknowledge each warning to continue.
- **Blockers**: problems you need to fix before you can downgrade. Acknowledging warnings doesn't clear blockers.

If there's nothing to remove, you'll see "No organization-only data was detected."

### 3. Confirm

Acknowledge what will be removed, then click **Downgrade to Teams**. To confirm, type your organization's name exactly as shown.

When it's done, you'll see "Organization downgraded to Teams".

---

## Before you downgrade

- If your organization has a paid subscription, you may need a payment method on file for the new Teams plan.
- Tell your members. Their teams, Links and bookings move according to the plan you chose.
