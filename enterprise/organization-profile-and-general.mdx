Organization owners and admins manage these under **Settings** > **Organization**. Members can open the Profile page, but only see a read-only summary.

## Profile

Go to **Settings** > **Organization** > [**Profile**](https://app.cal.com/settings/organizations/profile).

</Frame>

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Upload Logo | The logo shown on your organization's booking pages. |
| Upload logo for Cal Video | A separate logo for Cal Video calls. Use a light or white logo so it stands out on the dark background. |
| Upload banner | A banner image for your organization's public profile page. |
| Organization name | The name bookers see. |
| Organization URL | Your organization's subdomain, for example `acme.cal.com`. Click the copy icon to copy it. |
| Organization ID | Your organization's ID, which you may need for the API or when contacting support. |
| About | A few sentences about your organization, shown on its public profile. |
| Location | A free-text location, for example "London, UK". |

Click **Update** to save.

### Branding and visibility

Lower on the Profile page, you can set how all booking pages in your organization look:

- **Booking page theme**: light, dark or system.
- **Custom brand colors**: brand colors for the light and dark themes. Cal.com warns you if a color doesn't have enough contrast.
- **Corner style**: how rounded buttons and cards are.
- **Disable Cal.com branding**: removes the "Powered by Cal.com" badge.
- **Allow search engine indexing**: lets search engines show your organization's booking pages.
- **Disable public organization profile and redirect**: sends visitors of your organization URL to another address instead of showing your public profile.

## General

Go to **Settings** > **Organization** > [**General**](https://app.cal.com/settings/organizations/general) to set the organization's default **Timezone**, **Time format** (12-hour or 24-hour) and **Start of week**. Click **Update** to save.

Time format only changes how times show inside Cal.com, not on public booking pages.
