---
title: Complete your onboarding 
description: Once you create a Cal.com account, you are taken to the onboarding flow, where you'll need to go through the process of setting up some basics to get you up and running.
---

Onboarding takes a few minutes and has three steps: choose how you plan to use Cal.com, add your profile details, and connect a calendar. You can change everything you set here later in **Settings**.

## Select a plan

Choose what you plan to use Cal.com for, then click **Continue**:

- **For personal use** (free) to set up your own scheduling page.
- **With my team** to create a team and invite your teammates.
- **For my organization** to set up Cal.com for a larger company with more control, privacy, and security.

The rest of this article covers **For personal use**. If you pick a team or organization plan, you'll be guided through creating it next.

## Add your details

Set up the profile people see when they book with you:

- **Profile picture:** upload a photo so people know who they're booking with. The recommended size is 64x64px, up to 5 MB.
- **Your name:** the name shown on your booking page.
- **Username:** the end of your booking link, for example `cal.com/your-username`.
- **Bio:** a short description about yourself.

The preview on the right shows how your booking page will look as you type. Click **Continue** when you're done.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onboarding/add-your-details.png" alt="Add your details step with profile picture, name, username and bio fields next to a booking page preview" />
</Frame>

## Connect your calendar

Connect your calendar so Cal.com can check your busy times to prevent double bookings and add new bookings to your calendar as they're scheduled. Click **Connect** on the calendar you use and follow the prompts.

If you'd rather connect a calendar later, click **Skip for now**. You can connect one at any time from **Apps**. See [Calendar](/quick-start/calendar) for step-by-step guides for each calendar.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onboarding/connect-your-calendar.png" alt="Connect your calendar step with a list of calendar apps and a weekly calendar preview" />
</Frame>

## You're all set

When you finish, you'll see a welcome message and land on your dashboard, ready to start scheduling.
