## How to Connect Your Google Calendar

1. Go to **Settings** and click the [**Calendars**](https://app.cal.com/settings/my-account/calendars) tab.
2. Click **Add calendar**, or **Connect your first calendar** if you haven't connected one yet.
3. Choose **Google Calendar** and click **Install**.
4. Sign in with your Google account and allow the permissions Cal.com asks for, so it can read your busy times and add new bookings to your calendar.
5. Choose which calendars to check for conflicts and which calendar new bookings are added to. See [Calendars and conferencing](/my-account/calendars-and-conferencing).


## How to Connect Your Apple Calendar

Connecting your Apple Calendar helps Cal.com check when you're free or busy. This avoids double bookings and ensures new bookings are added to the right calendar.

Follow these steps:

1. **Log in to your Cal.com account**.

   * Go to **Settings** and click the [**Calendars**](https://app.cal.com/settings/my-account/calendars) tab.

2. **Add a Calendar**:

   * Click **Add Calendar**.

   * You’ll be taken to our App Store.

3. **Install the Calendar**:

   * Choose **Apple Calendar** and click **Install**.

4. **Log in with Your App-Specific Password** **and your APPLE ID Email.** 
  * Head over to https://appleid.apple.com/account/manage and generate your app-specific password.  
  * Paste the password in the password section.

5. **Set Up and Adjust Settings**:

   * You’ll see your calendar in the **Installed Calendars** section.

   * Select which calendar to check for conflicts.

   * Add more calendars if needed.

You're all set! Cal.com will now manage your availability and prevent scheduling
