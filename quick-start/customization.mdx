title: Customization
---

Use Appearance settings to choose how Cal.com looks in your dashboard and on booking pages.

## Appearance settings

1. Go to **Settings** > **My account** > **Appearance**.
2. In **Dashboard theme**, choose **System default**, **Light**, or **Dark**. This changes the logged-in dashboard, not your booking pages.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/customization/appearance.png" alt="Dashboard theme setting on the Appearance page" />
</Frame>

## Per-link overrides

1. Open **Links**, select a booking link, and choose **Appearance**.
2. Set options such as **Layout**, **Link color**, **Banner**, **Start of week**, and **Interface language**. Turn on **Lock timezone on booking page** to keep booking times in a specific timezone.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/timezone-lock/lock-timezone.png" alt="Appearance settings for a booking link, including the timezone lock" />
</Frame>

You can combine these settings with an [embed](https://cal.com/docs/core-features/embed) to make a Cal.com booking page feel like part of your website.
