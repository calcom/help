title: Customization

Use Appearance settings to choose how Cal.com looks in your dashboard and on booking pages.

## Appearance settings

1. Go to **Settings** > **My account** > **Appearance**.
2. In **Dashboard theme**, choose **System default**, **Light**, or **Dark**. This changes the logged-in dashboard, not your booking pages.
3. In **Booking page theme**, choose **System default**, **Light**, or **Dark**. This changes your public booking pages, not your dashboard.
4. In **Booking layout**, choose **Month**, **Weekly**, or **Column**. Bookers can switch between the layouts you select. Choose a **Default view** to set which layout opens first. The event type can override these settings.
5. In **Customize brand identity**, turn on **Enable custom brand colors** to set a **Brand color (light theme)** and **Brand color (dark theme)** for buttons and accents. Cal.com warns you if either color does not pass the contrast check. Under **Corner style**, choose **Sharp** or **Rounded**.
6. Turn on **Disable Cal.com branding** to remove the "Powered by Cal.com" badge and other Cal.com branding from your public booking pages. Availability depends on your plan.

## Per-link overrides

1. Open **Links**, select a booking link, and choose **Appearance**.
2. Set options such as **Layout**, **Link color**, **Banner**, **Start of week**, and **Interface language**. Turn on **Lock timezone on booking page** to keep booking times in a specific timezone.

You can combine these settings with an [embed](https://cal.com/docs/core-features/embed) to make a Cal.com booking page feel like part of your website.
