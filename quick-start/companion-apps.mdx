Cal.com Companion brings your Cal.com account to your phone and your browser. It's free with every Cal.com plan: sign in with your Cal.com account and your Links, bookings and availability are already there. See [cal.com/app](https://cal.com/app) for an overview.

## Mobile app

The Cal.com mobile app runs on iPhone and Android. From the app you can:

- Open a Link and copy it to share in any chat or email.
- See your bookings, join Cal Video, and reschedule or cancel a booking.
- Change your working hours, take a day off or switch schedules.
- Get a push notification when someone books. Choose which bookings notify you in [Notification preferences](/bookings/notification-preferences).

Download it from the [App Store](https://go.cal.com/iOS) or [Google Play](https://go.cal.com/android).

## Browser extension

The browser extension keeps your Links in the toolbar, so you can copy one into an email or a message without switching tabs. Install it for [Chrome](https://go.cal.com/chrome) or [Firefox](https://go.cal.com/firefox). For Safari and Microsoft Edge, join the waitlist at [cal.com/app](https://cal.com/app#platforms).

## Desktop app

The Cal.com desktop app runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. Download it from [cal.com/download](https://cal.com/download).
