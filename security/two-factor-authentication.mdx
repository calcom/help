Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a six-digit code from an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator or 1Password, every time you sign in. It keeps your account safe if your password is stolen.

Go to **Settings** > **Security** > [**Two factor auth**](https://app.cal.com/settings/security/two-factor-auth).

<Note>
  2FA needs an account password. If you signed up with Google or another sign-in provider and never set a password, click **Create account password** to go to the [Password](/security/security-settings#password) page and set one first.
</Note>

## Turn on two-factor authentication

1. Click **Enable**.
2. Confirm your current password and click **Continue**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the text code manually.
4. Enter the six-digit code from the app and click **Enable**.
5. Save your **Backup codes**. Click **Copy** or **Download** and keep them somewhere safe.

Each backup code works once and lets you sign in without your authenticator app.

## Turn off two-factor authentication

1. Click **Disable**.
2. Enter your password and the six-digit code from your authenticator app.
3. Click **Disable**.

If you no longer have your authenticator app, click **Lost access** and enter one of your backup codes instead.

We recommend turning 2FA back on as soon as you can.

## Signing in with 2FA

After you enter your email and password, Cal.com asks for your **Two-factor code**. If you don't have your phone, use a backup code. Repeated wrong codes count toward [account lockout](/security/account-lockout).
